Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use Siri to control my drives?

Dryves integrates seamlessly with Siri for hands-free drive management. Here's how to use it:

  • Start a drive: Say "Hey Siri, start a drive in Dryves"
  • Stop a drive: Say "Hey Siri, stop my drive in Dryves"
  • Check status: Say "Hey Siri, show my driving hours in Dryves"

You can also create custom Siri Shortcuts in the Shortcuts app for personalized voice commands.

How do I import driving hours from other apps?

Dryves makes it easy to import your existing driving data:

  • Open Dryves and go to Settings
  • Tap "Import Drives"
  • Select your previous app's export file (CSV or compatible format)
  • Review the imported drives and confirm

Your driving hours will be automatically added to your Dryves log with all metadata preserved.

How do location reminders work?

Dryves uses intelligent location-based reminders to help you remember to log your drives:

  • Save locations: Mark frequently visited places like home, school, or practice areas
  • Automatic detection: Dryves will detect when you arrive or leave these locations
  • Smart notifications: Get reminded to start or stop your drive at the right time
  • Privacy first: All location processing happens on your device

You can customize reminder settings in the app to match your preferences.

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

Your privacy is our top priority:

  • Local-first: All your driving data is stored locally on your device
  • Optional iCloud sync: Enable iCloud backup to sync across your Apple devices
  • No tracking: We don't track your location or share your data with third parties
  • Secure: All data is encrypted and protected by your device security

For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

How do Live Activities work?

Live Activities let you see your driving time in real-time on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (iPhone 14 Pro and later):

  • Start a drive in Dryves
  • The Live Activity automatically appears on your Lock Screen
  • See your drive duration, start time, and quick controls
  • On iPhone 14 Pro and later, the activity appears in the Dynamic Island

Live Activities require iOS 16.1 or later.

Can I use Dryves with CarPlay?

Yes! Dryves includes full CarPlay support:

  • Launch Dryves from your CarPlay dashboard
  • Start and stop drives with a single tap
  • View your current drive duration
  • All controls are optimized for in-car use and safety

CarPlay functionality requires iOS 16 or later and a compatible CarPlay system.

What widgets are available?

Dryves offers multiple widget options:

  • Home Screen widgets: Small, medium, and large widgets showing your progress
  • Lock Screen widgets: Circular and rectangular widgets for iOS 16+
  • StandBy mode: Full-screen widgets for iPhone 14 Pro and later
  • Quick actions: Start drives directly from widgets

To add a widget, long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen and tap the "+" button.
