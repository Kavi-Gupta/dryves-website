Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains how the Dryves app for iPhone (the "Application") and the dryves.app website handle information. Dryves is provided by Garun Gupta (the "Service Provider").

The short version

  • Your driving log is stored on your iPhone. The Service Provider cannot see it.
  • You don't create an account, and the Application doesn't ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The Application collects a limited amount of usage data for analytics. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Your personal information is not sold.

Information stored on your device

The Application keeps the following on your iPhone so that it can work:

  • Your drives: start and end times, duration, whether the drive was during the day or at night, and the weather.
  • Your hour goals and progress.
  • The places you save for reminders, such as home or school, and the reminder radius for each.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to the Service Provider. Depending on your Apple settings, it may be included in your device's backups, which are handled by Apple.

Usage data we collect

The Application collects usage data about how people interact with it, such as which features are used. This is used for analytics: to understand how the Application is used and to improve it. This usage data is not linked to your identity, and it does not include your driving log.

Location

If you grant permission, the Application uses your iPhone's location, including when the Application is not open, to:

  • remind you to start a drive when you leave a saved place, and to stop one when you arrive, or end the drive for you;
  • record the weather and time of day for a drive.

The Service Provider does not collect or store your location. To record the weather, the Application requests conditions from Apple's weather service, which requires sending the location of the drive to Apple. Using location in the background can reduce battery life. You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in the Settings app; reminders and automatic weather will stop working if you do.

Apple services

The Application works with Siri, Shortcuts, CarPlay, widgets, Live Activities, and notifications. These are provided by Apple, and requests you make through them, including voice requests, are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Tips and any other in-app purchases are processed by Apple. The Service Provider does not receive your payment details.

Imported logs

When you import drives from another app or from a CSV or PDF file, the Application reads the file you choose in order to add those drives to your log.

Who information is shared with

The Service Provider does not sell your personal information and does not share it for advertising. Usage data may be processed by service providers that supply analytics, acting on the Service Provider's behalf. Information may also be disclosed if the law requires it.

This website

dryves.app does not use cookies, advertising, or analytics scripts. The site is hosted by GitHub, which may record technical details of visits, such as IP addresses, in its server logs. If you email us, we keep your message and email address for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the conversation.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your driving log remains on your iPhone until you delete the drives or delete the Application. Deleting the Application removes the data it stored on your device. Because usage data is not linked to your identity, the Service Provider cannot look up or delete the usage data that came from a particular person.

Children and teens

Dryves is made for student drivers, many of whom are under 18. The Application is not directed to children under 13, and the Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact support@dryves.app so that it can be removed. If you are below the age at which you can consent to data processing where you live, a parent or guardian must agree to this policy on your behalf.

Security

Your driving log is protected by the security of your iPhone, including your passcode and Apple's device encryption. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, and the Service Provider cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continuing to use the Application after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@dryves.app.
