Common questions

Which devices does Dryves run on?
iPhone with iOS 17.0 or later. The drive timer in the Dynamic Island needs an iPhone 14 Pro or later; on other iPhones it appears on the Lock Screen.
Can I bring over drives from another app?
Yes. Dryves imports driving hours from RoadReady, and from CSV or PDF logs.
How do I start a drive with Siri?
Say “Hey Siri, start drive in Dryves.” You can end a drive or log a past one by voice too.
Where is my driving data stored?
On your iPhone. Dryves doesn't require an account. See the privacy policy for details.
I'm a parent. Do I need the app too?
No. Dryves runs on your teen's iPhone and keeps the log there. You can check progress toward day and night hours on the app's dashboard.
I run a driving school. How do we work with Dryves?
Schools can partner with us to monitor and track their students' driving practice. Write to partners@dryves.app and tell us a little about your school.
