Tracking every second 0:00

Logging hours shouldn't be the hard part of learning to drive.

Most driving logs are a paper sheet or a form you fill in after the fact. Dryves is built on the parts of iOS you already use, so starting a drive takes one tap or one sentence, and the details fill themselves in.

  • Hands-free with Siri

    Say “Hey Siri, start drive in Dryves.” End a drive or log a past one the same way, with your hands on the wheel.

  • Live Activities

    Your drive timer sits in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen. Stop the drive from there with one tap.

  • CarPlay

    Large Start and Stop buttons on the car's display, so the phone stays in your pocket.

  • Widgets

    Start or stop a drive from the Home Screen or Lock Screen without opening the app.

  • Location reminders

    Get a nudge when you leave home, school, or any saved place, and again when you're back. Set the radius for each one.

  • Weather, day, and night

    Every drive records the weather, and night hours are counted separately for states that require them.

  • Goals at a glance

    Set your own hour targets and watch total and night-time progress fill in on the dashboard.

  • Bring your old log

    Import your hours from RoadReady, or from a CSV or PDF, and keep going where you left off.

  • Private by default

    Your drives are stored on your iPhone. There's no account to create. Read the privacy policy.

Questions about logging driving hours

What is Dryves?
Dryves is a free driving logger for iPhone. Student drivers use it to keep a log of the supervised practice hours they need for a learner's permit or driver's license, with the date, length, weather, and day or night recorded for every drive.
Is Dryves free?
Yes. Dryves is free on the App Store, and there's no account to create.
Does Dryves track night driving hours?
Yes. Each drive is marked as day or night automatically, and night hours are counted separately on the dashboard for states that require them. You can set your own goals for total and night hours.
Can I log a drive I forgot to start?
Yes. You can log a past drive afterwards, by voice with Siri if you like. Location reminders also nudge you to start a drive when you leave home, school, or another saved place, so fewer drives slip through.
Can I move my hours from another driving log app?
Yes. Dryves imports driving hours from RoadReady, and from CSV or PDF logs, so you can keep going where you left off.
Which phones does Dryves work on?
Dryves is an iPhone app. It runs on iOS 17 or later, in English and Spanish.
